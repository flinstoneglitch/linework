How do I play?

Every level has two halves. First you plan while the world stands still, then you press GO and skate what you built.

  • Planning: tap a ramp, rail or bench to turn it to its next allowed direction. Drag it to slide it along the places it is allowed to sit (it snaps with a click). Dragging empty space nudges the camera a little. Press GO to lock the layout and start.
  • Skating: tap anywhere to pop (an ollie). Drag left or right to steer and let go to hold your line. A second finger gives an instant pop without lifting the steering finger. In the air, a quick flick left is a kickflip and right is a heelflip.
  • The lip of a ramp glows brighter as you approach it: that is when to pop.
  • The circular arrow button restores your starting layout while planning, and restarts the run while skating.

Failing is meant to be cheap. After a fall you are back to planning in about a second with your layout intact.

I'm stuck on a level

  • A jump falls short: look at where you placed the ramp. The place that looks obvious is not always the one that gives you enough speed. Try a different spot, and try turning it.
  • Level 7, Wrong Way Up: you can turn the view with the slider at the bottom of the screen. Each gap has a pair of posts topped with glowing dots, joined by a thin beam. Turn the view until a pair's two dots slide together into one, hold still for a moment, and a bridge of light locks in. One pair is a decoy.
  • Level 8, The Line: the line is too long to see at once, so pinch to zoom and drag to pan, or tap the numbered buttons to jump to each ramp. Fly through the gate of light and flick sideways in the air to unfold the gantry.
  • Cassette tapes are optional. Up to three are hidden on levels 2 to 9, and you never need them to finish.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Linework works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your device only; there are no accounts and no cloud sync. If you restore your new phone from a device backup (iCloud or a computer), your progress comes with it. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings from the main menu and choose Reset all progress.

I can't hear any sound

Linework follows your phone's silent switch and Ringer setting, so check that first, then the music and effects volumes in the game's Settings. The game plays politely alongside other apps' audio.

Accessibility

In Settings you can change the music and effects volume, turn haptics off, turn on Reduce Motion (the game also follows your iPhone's Reduce Motion setting), turn on High Contrast (it also follows Increase Contrast), turn on sound captions, and choose which side the GO button sits on. Nothing in the game depends on telling colours apart: objects you can move have an outline, a pulse and an arrow as well as colour. With VoiceOver you can arrange the objects in each level, but the skating itself is a timing game and is not designed to be played with VoiceOver. If something gets in your way, please tell me.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iOS 17 or later.
