Linework

Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

The short version

Linework is an iPhone game made by Colin Oreilly at Artphorm ("we", "us"). We do not collect, store, transmit, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game does not use the internet.

Information we collect

None. Linework does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else. We do not run any servers for the game, and the app never asks for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal data. It does not ask for any permissions at all.

What stays on your device

To remember where you are, the game saves a small file on your device, in the app's own storage. It contains:

  • which levels you have finished, your best times and your number of attempts;
  • which cassette tapes you have picked up and which music tracks you have unlocked;
  • how you last arranged the ramps, rails and other objects on each level;
  • which on-screen hints you have already seen;
  • your settings: music and effects volume, haptics, reduced motion, high contrast, captions and left- or right-handed layout.

This data is used only to run the game. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

Backups

Like other app data, this file may be included in a backup of your device (for example an iCloud or computer backup) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are created and managed by Apple under your device settings and Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive them.

Third parties, advertising and tracking

Linework contains no third-party code libraries, advertising networks, analytics services or trackers. There are no in-app purchases. We do not share or sell data to anyone, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Because Linework collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Data retention and deletion

  • Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Deleting Linework deletes all of it.
  • You can also erase your progress at any time in the game: Settings ▸ Reset all progress.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we delete them on request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
