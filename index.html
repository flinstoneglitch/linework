Build the line.
Skate it.

A surreal isometric skateboard puzzle game for iPhone.

The world holds still while you plan. Turn a ramp, slide a rail, line up an impossible view, then press GO and skate the line you built. If it goes wrong you are back to planning in about a second, with your layout intact.

  • Nine hand-built levels, from a quiet plaza to a dawn finale over the water.
  • Ramps, rails, benches, wall rides, machines that wake when you grind, and bridges that only exist from the right angle.
  • Original music for every level, made to sit under the sound of the board.
  • No ads, no accounts, no purchases, and no internet connection needed.
  • Made to be playable by more people: reduced motion, high contrast, captions, adjustable haptics and either-hand controls.
